A Book by Bradley Hobbs

Kept or Released by Covenant

Discovering what belongs to the covenant, what belongs to culture, and what we may need to release.

Faith deserves to be approached with honesty, reverence, and a willingness to examine what we have inherited. This book invites readers to consider the difference between the covenant God established and the customs, expectations, and boundaries that people have built around it.

This is not an invitation to abandon faith or dishonor the generations who came before us. It is an invitation to look again through the life and teachings of Jesus, holding tightly to what is rooted in Him and being willing to release what is not.
